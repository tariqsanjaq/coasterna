## Known Landmines Check

### 1. `ElevatedButton` inside a `Row` without `minimumSize: const Size(0, 48)`

**Result: CLEAN — 0 violations. No new ones introduced by Task #14.**

All 6 real `ElevatedButton` call sites in `lib/`, each checked for its actual parent:

| Location | Parent | `minimumSize` | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `admin_manage_screen.dart:73` | **`Row`** | present, `Size(0, 48)` at `:85` | Safe |
| `admin_manage_screen.dart:324` | **`Row`** (dialog actions, `:310`) | present, `Size(0, 48)` at `:331` | Safe |
| `admin_form_widgets.dart:249` | **`Row`** | present, `Size(0, 48)` at `:254` | Safe |
| `home_page.dart:298` | `SizedBox(width: double.infinity, height: kMinTouchTarget)` at `:295-297` | not needed | Safe |
| `admin_login_screen.dart:173` | `SizedBox(width: double.infinity, height: kMinTouchTarget)` at `:170-172` | not needed | Safe |
| `trip_details_screen.dart:188` | `SizedBox(width: double.infinity)` at `:186` | not needed | Safe |

The three `Row` cases all carry the guard, with the reasoning documented inline at
`admin_manage_screen.dart:78-85` (a `Row` measures a non-flex child against unbounded
width, and the theme's `Size.fromHeight` — "as wide as possible" — has no answer against
infinity, so it throws). Non-`ElevatedButton` siblings in `Row`s are guarded too:
